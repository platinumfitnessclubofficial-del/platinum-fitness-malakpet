Questions

Before you walk in.

What does the ₹500 trial pass include?
One full day of access to the gym floor, trainer guidance during your visit, locker and shower. The ₹500 is adjusted against any membership you take within 7 days, so the trial works out free if you join.
How do the spot offers work?
Today's Spot Offers: 3 months at ₹9,999 (was ₹12,000), 6 months at ₹11,999 (was ₹17,000) and 12 months at ₹17,999 (was ₹23,000). Charm prices, valid today only, first come first served, before tonight's deadline. Contact the desk to block your slot.
Can I freeze, transfer or refund my membership?
No. All memberships are non-transferable, non-refundable and cannot be frozen. Please choose your duration based on your schedule.
How many times can I enter the gym per day?
Membership includes one entry per day. If you leave and want to return the same day, speak to the front desk — repeat entry is not guaranteed under the standard membership.
Is the steam bath really included every week?
Yes. Every membership includes one steam bath session per week. Additional sessions are not part of the standard plan.
Do I get a personal trainer?
A general trainer is included on the floor for everyone, along with exercise card planning and 7 days of guided onboarding. Dedicated one-on-one personal training is a separate arrangement — ask at the desk.
What are the timings and weekly off?
Monday to Saturday, 6:00 AM to 11:00 PM. The gym is closed on Sundays. Plan your week around one rest day at the club.
Is the gym unisex?
Yes — Platinum Fitness Club Unisex in Malakpet welcomes men and women on the same floor, including couples and families training together.
